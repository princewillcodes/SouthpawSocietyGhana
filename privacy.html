Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Southpaw Society Ghana handles stories submitted through this website.

Information we collect

When you submit a story, we collect your name, email address, role or relationship to the story, and the story you provide. We use these details to receive and review your submission and to contact you if needed.

How submissions are stored and who can access them

The form sends submissions to a Google Apps Script, which records them in a Google Sheet. Access to submissions is limited to authorised Southpaw Society Ghana representatives. The society has advised that only two representatives currently have access. Submissions are kept for 12 months from the date they are submitted, then removed from the active submissions database.

How stories may be shared

If you agree to the story consent shown with the form, Southpaw Society Ghana may publish your story anonymously on its social media or use it during outreach campaigns. Your name and email address will not be included with a published story. We collect those details so we can manage your submission and contact you if needed.

Your choices and questions

You can contact Southpaw Society Ghana at southpawsocietyghana@gmail.com with questions about your submission or to ask for it to be removed. Please include enough information for us to locate it. We will review your request and respond.

Third-party services

Google services are used to receive and store story submissions. Google may process information under its own terms and privacy practices.

For questions about this notice, contact Southpaw Society Ghana at the email address above.
