Unlearning the Stigma

Click the cards to reveal the scientific truth behind common cultural myths.

Cultural Myth ❌

"Using the left hand to give, receive, or eat is intentionally disrespectful."

Tap to debunk
Scientific Reality ✅

Handedness is completely biological. Respect comes from the heart and intent, not which hemisphere of the brain controls your dominant hand.

Cultural Myth ❌

"Left-handedness can be corrected if a child is forced to switch early."

Tap to debunk
Scientific Reality ✅

Forcing a shift causes severe neurological confusion, leading to learning delays, stuttering, and emotional frustration. It should never be "corrected".
